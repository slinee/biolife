Field guide

Resource ecology

Each cell consumes local nutrients, stores energy, pays maintenance, and ages. Empty substrate can receive offspring from a neighbor with sufficient energy. Parents pay for reproduction. Three lineages compete cyclically: algae replaces coral, coral replaces spores, and spores replace algae. Replacement retains 80% of the consumed cell's energy. Dead cells return part of their energy to nutrients.

Nutrients regenerate toward a maximum of 1. Stored energy is bounded by 2. Cells die from starvation or maximum age. These are exploratory artificial-life rules, not a predictive biological model.

Reproducible experiments

The seed determines initial conditions and every stochastic transition. A habitat checkpoint saves the full random-generator state and all resource arrays. Restoring it continues the same experiment. The batch worker executes independent runs with consecutive seeds, samples measurements, and aggregates each run. CSV files contain measurements; JSON also includes the settings and run status.

Classic rules

Conway is exactly B3/S23, HighLife is B36/S23, and Seeds is B2/S∅. Resource dynamics are disabled in these modes. The 150 × 100 grid wraps at its edges. All updates are discrete generations.
